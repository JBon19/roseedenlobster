BON TEMPS BOILS
Cajun Catering & On-Site Boils
Bon Temps Boils brings the full Louisiana seafood boil experience to your event — anywhere in Maine. We show up, set up, and throw down a proper boil your guests won't forget. 
Private Events
Backyard parties, rehearsal dinners, family reunions — whatever the occasion, we'll bring the crawfish, shrimp, corn, potatoes, and all the fixings. Fully on-site. No kitchen needed.
Weddings & Large Parties
Looking for something different? A Cajun boil is a show and a meal in one. We handle the cooking, the setup, and the good times. You just show up hungry.
Corporate & Community
Team events, fundraisers, festivals — we scale to fit. Whether it's 30 people or 300, we've got you covered with authentic Louisiana flavor.
Custom Menus
Lobster boils, crawfish boils, shrimp boils, or a mix of everything. We'll work with you to build the perfect spread for your crew.
