Come Sit Down

Yes, You Can
Eat Here Too.

Rose Eden Lobster is a full seafood shack — not just catering. Pull off Route 3, grab a picnic table under our covered patio, and dig into fresh Maine seafood served the way it's meant to be.

🍦 Fresh Local Seafood

Live Maine lobsters, steamer clams, mussels, and whatever the boats brought in that morning. Sourced from local Downeast fishermen and cooked to order.

☂ Covered Outdoor Dining

All the charm of a Maine roadside lobster shack without the weather stress. Our outdoor seating is fully covered — rain or shine, you stay dry and still get the salt-air experience.

🚗 Walk Up, Order, Dig In

No reservations, no fuss. Casual picnic-table dining the way a Maine seafood shack should be. Takeout is available too if you're headed back to your rental.

⛰ On the Way to Acadia

Perfectly located on Route 3, just minutes from Acadia National Park. Stop in on your way to the carriage roads — or reward yourself after a long hike with a lobster roll.
